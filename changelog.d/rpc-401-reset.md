Fixed

- **A refused JSON-RPC request gets its answer, not a connection reset.** A 401, 404, or 405 went out before the request body was read. When the body arrived after the headers, the close reset the connection, and the client could read `ECONNRESET` instead of the answer. mempool re-reads a rotated cookie only on that 401. The node now reads and drops a body no handler read before it answers, up to 64 KiB and the 30 s read timeout. A broken body is then 400 and a stalled one 408, the status an authorized request already gets, and an authorized request's 400 for a broken body now closes the connection too. A larger body, declared or streamed, gets its answer at once with `Connection: close`, and the node reads no more of it. A client that sends such a body with the headers can still see a reset instead of the answer, as with an authorized 413. A body held back for `Expect: 100-continue` is answered at once with `Connection: close`.
